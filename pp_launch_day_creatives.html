The feature every coach said they didn't ask for — but now can't imagine not having. We launched 48 hours ago. In that time, the feature that's generated the most messages isn't the workout builder or the payments system. It's the churn risk score. Every client on Peak Performance gets a weekly engagement score. When it drops below a threshold — overdue check-ins, declining workout completions, reduced messaging — the platform flags it. Three coaches have already messaged us to say it alerted them to a client about to go quiet. They reached out. The client came back. That's the difference between a tool and a platform that thinks with you. We're two days in and already seeing the value we set out to build.
